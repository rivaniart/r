A GALLERY FOR CONSIDERED COLLECTING

Rivani brings distinctive fine art into the rooms where life actually happens.

Based in Mumbai, Rivani curates original paintings, heritage textiles, and relief sculptures for architectural residences across India. We believe art should be experienced in dialogue with spatial light, natural textures, and the rhythms of daily living.

ORIGINAL WORKS Authenticated original paintings & dimensional reliefs tailored for considered architectural residences.
ARCHIVAL FRAMING Handcrafted teak, walnut, and gold leaf framing included with every acquisition.
PRIVATE VIEWINGS In-home viewings across Mumbai and insured white-glove transit throughout India.
